Asset Lifecycle: From Procurement to Disposal

The 5 phases of asset life and where organizations leak money.

10 March 2026 11 min read SCORP Research

Asset lifecycle management determines the financial performance of industrial operations. Procurement, deployment, maintenance and disposal introduce different cost drivers.

Many organizations focus only on maintenance while ignoring the earlier phases where most asset value is actually lost.

Key Insight

Organizations often lose up to 30% of asset value during procurement and deployment before the asset even reaches stable operation.

Procurement Phase

Vendor selection, specification accuracy and warranty coverage directly determine lifecycle economics.

Deployment Phase

Installation quality and early operational monitoring determine long-term reliability.

The Five Phases of Asset Lifecycle

Procurement

Acquire assets from vendors and suppliers.

Deployment

Install, calibrate and integrate the asset.

Operation

Asset performs its intended operational role.

Maintenance

Preventive and corrective servicing.

Disposal

Retirement, resale or recycling.

80%

of maintenance budgets remain reactive rather than preventive.

Conclusion

Organizations that understand lifecycle economics gain a significant operational advantage. Instead of reacting to failures, they proactively manage asset performance from acquisition to retirement.
