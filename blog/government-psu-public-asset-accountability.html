Government departments, PSUs, municipal corporations manage ₹50 lakh crore public assets — hospital equipment, school labs, railway signalling, irrigation pumps, water treatment plants, street lighting, PSU manufacturing lines. Public scrutiny intense: CAG performance audits, RTI asset status queries, Lok Sabha questions on breakdown frequency. Asset registers outdated. Maintenance paper-based. No accountability trail.

EAM delivers audit-defensible records. This covers public sector compliance needs and accountability enforcement.

· · ·
Section Image  ·  Unique Scrutiny of Public Assets  ·  16 : 9

Unique Scrutiny of Public Assets

CAG 16-point check: Asset register complete? PM schedules followed? Repairs economical? Utilisation optimal? EAM provides evidence.

RTI: 'How many dialysis machines functional in govt hospitals?' Manual reconciliation takes weeks. EAM answers instantly.

Section Image  ·  Accountability Gaps in Manual Systems  ·  16 : 9

Accountability Gaps in Manual Systems

Paper logbooks vanish. Excel files on personal laptops. No digital trail linking work order to technician to supervisor approval. CAG flags 'undocumented maintenance expenditure ₹X crore'.

Section Image  ·  EAM: Digital Accountability Trail  ·  16 : 9

EAM: Digital Accountability Trail

Every asset registered with purchase order, commissioning date. Work orders require geo-fencing, photo before/after, supervisor approval.

  • CAG extracts: One-click PM compliance 98%
  • RTI portal integration: Real-time asset status
  • Vigilance clearance: Immutable audit trail

Section Image  ·  Multi-Department Coordination  ·  16 : 9

Multi-Department Coordination

PWD handles civil, electrical wing handles generators, biomedical dept handles medical equipment. EAM unifies visibility across departments.

Section Image  ·  Budget Defence with Data  ·  16 : 9

Budget Defence with Data

Justify ₹10 crore replacement budget with asset age, breakdown frequency, repair cost trends exceeding 70% asset value.

Section Image  ·  Government Scheme Asset Tracking  ·  16 : 9

Government Scheme Asset Tracking

Jal Jeevan Mission pumps, PM Awas Yojana solar panels tagged to scheme codes. Utilisation reporting to MoF.

Section Image  ·  Public Vendor Performance Tracking  ·  16 : 9

Public Vendor Performance Tracking

AMC vendor SLAs enforced. Blacklisting based on repeat failures. Transparent empanelment renewal.
