ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) is a broad system managing financials, HR, procurement, and operations. EAM (Enterprise Asset Management) focuses specifically on the lifecycle, maintenance, and cost of physical assets. Sapphire is both — it manages the full asset lifecycle (EAM) while integrating the financial, organisational, and operational layers (ERP) into one system. You don't need to buy two platforms.

SAP and Oracle require months of implementation, dedicated consultants, and IT teams to configure and maintain. Sapphire is designed for deployment in days, not months — and for use by the operations team directly, not a specialist IT department. It's purpose-built for asset-intensive operations in India, including Indian depreciation compliance under Company Act 2013 and IT Act 1961 which larger platforms handle poorly without costly customisation.

The Sapphire mobile application supports offline operation. Inspections can be conducted, work orders can be updated, and data can be logged without an active connection. All changes sync automatically when connectivity is restored — with timestamps preserved from the time the action was taken, not the time it synced.

Sapphire's bulk entry system is specifically designed for large onboarding events. Shared attributes are captured once and cascaded across hundreds of units. For most operations, an asset inventory of 200–500 items can be onboarded within a single working day by one person. Most clients are operational within 72 hours of starting onboarding.
